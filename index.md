# Kelp

A video app for a server you run yourself.

Kelp is an iPhone, iPad and Mac client for your own Yattee or Invidious server. It gives you your
subscriptions, your feed, trending and popular, channels, playlists, search and comments, with a
player built for watching rather than for scrolling.

## You need your own server

**Kelp does not work on its own and there is no service behind it.** You host a Yattee or
Invidious server, point Kelp at its address in Settings, and that is the whole of the setup. There
is nothing to sign up for and no account to create.

If you do not have a server yet, Kelp opens with a **Look Around First** button that fills the app
with sample content and plays a short clip, with no server and no network at all, so you can see
what it is before setting anything up.

## What it does

- Your subscriptions and feed, with optional new-upload badges.
- Trending and Popular, by region.
- Search, with suggestions, filters and channel results.
- Channels, playlists and comments.
- A player that handles up to 4K at 60 frames a second, with captions, chapters, an audio
  language picker and background audio. HDR on iPhone and iPad.
- SponsorBlock skipping, which is told a partial hash and never which video you are watching.
- Watch history and Continue Watching, kept on your device.
- Import and export of everything, as one file.

## What it does not do

- **No advertising.** None, and no affiliate links.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry. The app sends nothing to its
  developer, ever.
- **No account.** Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.
- **No subscription, no paid tiers, no in-app purchases.** Kelp is free and stays free.

## Privacy

The short version is that Kelp collects nothing and sends nothing to its developer. The longer
version, including the two optional services it can talk to and the one thing your server's
configuration decides for you, is on the [privacy page](privacy.html).
